ABOUT THE NEWSLETTER

unsent letters.

Send me a quiet confession. Dilemmas that keep you awake, unspoken feelings, the truths that live only in your head. I don't advise severely; I'm not qualified for that. But I can read, reflect, and remind you (& me) — it's not just you. It's us.

— it's michelle d.

HOW IT WORKS

three steps. one letter.

01

write it down

Whatever it is. The thing you've been circling. The question you're scared to ask out loud. Write it here — no name, no context needed.

02

send it anonymously

All submissions are completely anonymous. I read every single one. Nothing is dismissed, nothing is judged. Very brave of you, genuinely.

03

keep an eye out

I publish one or two letters a week on the newsletter. Yours might be next. Subscribe so you don't miss it when it lands.

A few gentle guidelines

Please only share writing you’re comfortable submitting for possible publication.

  • Keep it personal, reflective, and rooted in your own voice.
  • Avoid sharing private identifying details about other people.
  • You may sign your name, initials, or remain anonymous.

By submitting, you understand your letter may be considered for publication.
